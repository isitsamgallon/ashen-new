# Session Summary 
The session opened with the party processing the shocking death of Denola, with Halfbie panicking over their body being used as the instrument of murder. Mordecai secretly felt relief but masked it, while Viktor and Drache shared the guilt of Myst's death during their ill-fated trip to Whitespire. The party split up to break the news to the remaining New Gods: Mordecai and Halfbie found Cas, who accepted the news with unsettling equanimity, revealing her awareness of her own mortality. Halfbie performed a puppet show for Cas that subtly conveyed their own feelings of being manipulated, while Mordecai sang a song emphasizing the party were not villains. Meanwhile, Viktor and Drache found Seer in a pub, drunk and speaking in fragmented sentences, another sign that Myst's death had broken something in him.

That night, Viktor caught Chenris writing on Halfbie's walls again and bargained for information: Chenris revealed that Haldir wanted the New God's body as a vessel to contain his immense magical power, as he maintains the global portal network and Strixhaven's protections. In exchange, Viktor attempted to scry on Ebis Daro using a book Chenris provided, but Ebis detected the spell, sending a piercing glare that filled Viktor with dread. As the party gathered to discuss the situation, warning bells rang out: Ebis's forces were attacking Blackwall, led by a massive aspect and an army of Ash Spawn. Chenris took control of Halfbie's body to coordinate the city's defenses with tactical brilliance, creating a trebuchet and repositioning troops, as the party steeled themselves for what they knew would be an incredibly difficult fight.
