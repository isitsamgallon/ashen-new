# Session Summary 
The battle against the Aspect was a desperate struggle: Chenris used dimension door to engage it directly, while the party was surrounded by exploding Ash Spawn. After the aspect fell, it re-formed as a shapeshifter, mimicking Mordecai and then Drache, sowing confusion among the party. Eventually, Mordecai (with Seifer's help) delivered the killing blow using a twinned Chromatic Orb, but the victory was costly, the party was exhausted, and the war against the endless Ash Spawn continued to rage around them. Seifer emerged to mock their performance, and Chenris created a Leomund's Tiny Hut for a desperate rest, but their respite was shattered by an overwhelming wave of power emanating from the battlefield.

Viktor, separated from the party during the retreat, was confronted by Ebis Daro himself. Ebis effortlessly transformed the fallen soldiers into hundreds of new Ash Spawn, then forcibly extracted the psychopomp from Viktor's cloak and corrupted it into an ash-and-lava servant. Ebis spared Viktor's life, dismissing him as insignificant, and departed through a portal with his forces. Viktor returned to Blackwall to find the city in flames and the party tending to the wounded, haunted by the devastation and the terrifying realization that their victory over the aspect meant nothing, Ebis had grown stronger from their losses. The session ended with Viktor wondering aloud how anyone could possibly defeat a foe who turns every defeat into fuel for his army.
