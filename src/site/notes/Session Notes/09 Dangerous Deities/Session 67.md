# Session Summary 
The session opened in Blackwall's aftermath, with Viktor proposing a magical solution to prevent Ebis from raising the dead, but Chenris deemed it impractical. That night, Drache dreamed of the Korrigan, who tasked him with finding Marcel in the Deadlands, a favor Drache reluctantly accepted. The next morning, Chenris (still in control of Halfbie's body) secretly altered their teleportation coordinates to the Deadlands instead of Camp Sophia. Upon arrival, the party discovered a church dedicated to Chronovorus containing a massive time rift. Viktor and Chenris peered through the rift, witnessing Marcel's origin story and the brutal history of the Black Cult. Viktor eventually found a hidden chamber containing a tower-crystal, where Terathera's voice spoke to him cryptically.

Chenris revealed his true plan: to become a god and kill Aeloria. He absorbed the crystal's power, forming his own divine domain, and struck a deal with Chronovorus for assistance. Aeloria appeared to confront him, but Chronovorus opened time rifts, bringing Xekai, Niaros, and a young Marcel to the battle. Viktor sacrificed his life force to power a crystal device that forced Aeloria into physical form, allowing Chenris to destroy her. Chenris ascended to godhood, using his new power to resurrect Viktor and Halfbie, but Halfbie woke up disoriented, frantically calling for something called Gil-Galad.
