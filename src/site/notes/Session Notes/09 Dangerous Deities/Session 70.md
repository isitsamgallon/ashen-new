# Session Summary 
Following the contract signing, the party found The Hand dead, drained of all life, and discovered that Marcel's awakening had killed most of his clan. Viktor attempted to negotiate with Marcel for weapons and information, but after provoking the ancient vampire, he was bitten, ordered to cut off his own leg, and thrown from the room. Meanwhile, Halfbie explored the caves, found the dying Mouth, who revealed he had kept Marcel asleep for thousands of years because "he can't be trusted," and learned that if Vaelrith dies, Marcel would become far more powerful. The Mouth gave Halfbie a parchment containing a complex ritual to put Marcel back to sleep and then passed away peacefully. Halfbie also found a shadowy longsword in the armoury, but upon identifying it, they were transported into Marcel's body, realizing Marcel had swapped into theirs.

The party gradually uncovered the switch, and a chaotic series of body-swaps followed via the sword, with Marcel briefly inhabiting Drache and Mordecai's bodies while mocking them. The party managed to knock Halfbie's body unconscious to force Marcel out, but discovered his curse would simply reincarnate him into someone else if the body died. Halfbie eventually convinced Marcel to switch back by promising him "a night of fun," and the session ended with Halfbie blindfolding and cuffing Marcel in his room, preparing to escape.
