# Session Summary 
The party fled through a cramped tunnel into a hidden vampire enclave, following a hooded figure with a sewn-shut mouth. They met a council of deformed vampires, The Mouth, The Eyes, The Ears, The Hands, and [[Named Characters & Beasts/The Manner\|The Manner]], who revealed they had been waiting for the party and granted them an audience with "the original." After a long wait, Korrigan appeared through Drache's shadow and cursed him with uncontrollable bodily functions until he opened the heavy wooden door. Inside, they found Marcel suspended in an ornate coffin sustained by pipes carrying blood. The room itself was hostile: Drache and Viktor couldn't breathe, Mordecai was violently pulled in and knocked unconscious by a telepathic scream, and the party quickly realized Marcel was far more powerful than they had anticipated.

When Drache delivered Korrigan's message, that Vaelrith had returned, Marcel's eyes snapped open, the pipes burst, and he emerged from his coffin. He immediately recognized Viktor as the one who took the crystal and Halfbie as present at the church's destruction. After snapping off one of Mordecai's horns for withholding information, Marcel learned that Chenris had ascended to godhood by killing Aeloria. Enraged, he demanded to know who the party's strongest member was before driving his hand into Drache's gut and producing five magical contracts, each with a single clause: "The Fatebreakers will kill Vaelrith." With no other choice, the party signed, though Halfbie paused before signing, a smile flickering across their face.
