# Session Summary 
The session opened in the aftermath of Chenris's apotheosis, with Viktor revealing he had successfully transformed himself into one of the tower-crystals, his body now covered in red crystalline growths and his eyes turned gold, effectively making him a mobile anti-god field. Halfbie woke up disoriented, their mannerisms different from both their old self and Chenris, and they vaguely remembered the name Gil-Galad, which Viktor warned them not to pursue. Mordecai stepped outside the party's Leomund's Tiny Hut to confirm that Seifer was still present, relieved to find she had merely been unconscious during the divine battle, though he warned her presence would fade if Viktor remained too close.

That night, the party was surrounded by hundreds of vampires from the Crimson Vanguard, led by a 7-foot-tall brute who refused to negotiate. After a tense standoff, a mysterious woman in a red satin dress walked straight through the Tiny Hut's barrier, revealing herself as the vampire queen who had helped the party earlier. Halfbie seized the opportunity to cast Geas on her, commanding her to take the tribe to Merrihallows. Though the spell couldn't force her to her death, she reluctantly attacked her own followers to compel their obedience, creating chaos that let the party escape to the church. There, an old wizard appeared in a newly manifested corridor, ushering them to safety as the vampire horde descended, and the corridor vanished behind them, leaving the party in darkness.
