# Session Summary
After Halfbie's brief and disappointing encounter with Marcel, Mordecai stepped in, using their experience from the pleasure district, to finish the job in exchange for everyone being returned to their correct bodies. Once restored, the party escaped the caves and found Selene, the mysterious vampire woman, lying dead from the earlier battle. When Halfbie tried to leave her a note, she revived and agreed to meet them for "coffee" (blood) in her hometown of La Garde De Nuit, revealing her mutation allows her to hijack and control others' magic at the cost of her own spellcasting. That night, Selene slipped away to meet a cloaked figure, and when Viktor and Drache followed, they returned to find the Tiny Hut had vanished, leaving the party exposed.

Halfbie's dream transported them through a nightmare of abandonment and rejection, where their "friends" chose Chenris over them and left them to the Ash Spawn. They were rescued by Somnora, a fellow "replacement," who explained that lost souls here confront their greatest fears. Halfbie awoke with new clarity, reactivated the barrier, and cryptically announced that "Somnora is an Ace of Spades." The next morning, Halfbie fabricated a parasol for Selene (which she hijacked and adorned with gold), and the party set off toward Camp Sophia, stopping at an ancient abandoned temple called Le Deuxieme Fils. There they found a bottomless well, a vault of cursed gold meant for scientific research, and a skeleton with journals in the vampire tongue—one noting that Marcel was expected to visit and check on the research, though the writer feared the progenitor would not be pleased with their progress.
