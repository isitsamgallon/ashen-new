### Overview
 The [[Other Information/Races/Vampire\|Vampire]] noble first seen in [[Session Notes/04 Ignatius Beckons/Session 38\|Session 38]] when [[The Party/Halfbie\|Halfbie]] unexpectedly became the barer of [[Gods & Heralds/Vaelrith/Vaelrith\|Vaelrith]]. She then later apperaer when [[Other Information/Display Tags/The Party\|the party]] was sournouned by the [[Groups & Factions/Crimson Vanguard\|Crimson Vanguard]] in [[Session Notes/09 Dangerous Deities/Session 68\|Session 68]]. She was killed by the battle company after [[The Party/Halfbie\|Halfbie]] cast [[Admin/Spells/geas\|geas]] on her to make her help them. 

### Description
#### Appearance
She wears a long red satin dress. she has black hair with a tiara with a blood vile pendent attached to it. She rides a large skeletal horse

#### Ability
She has a the unique ability to quickly analysis the magic she sees and create the perfect counter to each spell as long as she has the time to properly figure it out first. She first showed this ability in [[Session Notes/09 Dangerous Deities/Session 68\|Session 68]] when she was able to walk straight through [[The Party/Halfbie\|Halfbie]]'s [[Admin/Spells/Leomund's Tiny Hut\|Leomund's Tiny Hut]] with ease.
