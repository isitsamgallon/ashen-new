One of the vampires met in [[Session Notes/09 Dangerous Deities/Session 69\|session 69]] when [[Other Information/Display Tags/The Party\|the party]] were invited in to [[Locations/The Deadlands/Marcel's Crypt\|Marcel's Crypt]]. The Eyes belongs to a group of maimed vampires named after various body parts.
