### Overview
Ebis Daro is a mysterious and immensely powerful antagonist encountered within the Ashen Campaign setting. A master shapeshifter and a figure of considerable magical might, he is suspected of being affiliated with [[Gods & Heralds/Ignatius/Ignatius\|Ignatius]] and [[Groups & Factions/Religious Organisations/The Ashen Cult\|the Ashen Cult]], and may be connected to the legend of [[Books, Documents & Artefacts/The Unbroken Coin - A Tale of Two Brothers\|The Unbroken Coin - A Tale of Two Brothers]]. He commands legions of [[Gods & Heralds/Ignatius/Ash Spawn\|Ash Spawn]] and deploys magical constructs known as Aspects to carry out his will.

### Description
Ebis Daro is a being of profound magical ability, capable of shape-shifting into numerous forms and [[Other Information/Races/Races\|races]]. He wields the [[Books, Documents & Artefacts/Artefacts/Firesoul Greatsword\|Firesoul Greatsword]], an ornate blade that ignites upon drawing, and has demonstrated the power to convert fallen soldiers into [[Gods & Heralds/Ignatius/Ash Spawn\|Ash Spawn]], forcibly extract and corrupt psychopomps, and detect scrying attempts from vast distances

He is known to deploy Aspects magical copies of himself that function as proxies. These constructs can shapeshift when damaged and, while formidable, do not harm Ebis himself upon their destruction. Ebis appears to draw strength from [[Other Information/Display Tags/The Party\|the party]]'s victories, as their defeat of his Aspect only seemed to fuel his army.

#### Personality
Ebis Daro is characterised by immense arrogance and a cruel, dismissive demeanour. He regards others with contempt, referring to [[The Party/Viktor Malkovich\|Viktor]] as a "tick" with "no bearing on the schemes of the lion above it". He delights in displays of power, such as effortlessly transforming an entire battlefield of the fallen into his own soldiers, and takes what he wants without negotiation, "I take what I want".

Despite his brutality, he is not without a certain calculated pragmatism; he spared [[The Party/Viktor Malkovich\|Viktor]]'s life, deeming him unworthy of the effort to kill, and used him as a messenger to spread fear. [[Named Characters & Beasts/Important Characters/Seifer Willow ‡\|Seifer]], who fought alongside him during [[History & Events/The Sundering\|the Sundering]], described him as "weird" and "always a creep".

#### Appearance
When encountered directly during the Battle of [[Locations/OFC/Blackwall/Blackwall\|Blackwall]], Ebis Daro appeared as a figure enveloped in a burgundy red mantle with fine gold detailing that covered his body from head to toe. His face was obscured by a simple gold mask. He carried the [[Books, Documents & Artefacts/Artefacts/Firesoul Greatsword\|Firesoul Greatsword]], which burst into flames upon being drawn. He was accompanied by a young girl wearing a similar gold mask.

His presence exuded an overwhelming aura of power; [[The Party/Viktor Malkovich\|Viktor]], upon beholding him, felt an instinctive urge to kneel.

### Notable Information
- Shapeshifting: Ebis Daro is a prolific shapeshifter. Known forms he has taken include the leader of [[Groups & Factions/Religious Organisations/The Red Cult\|the Red Cult]], a dwarf Grandmaster teaching [[Named Characters & Beasts/Important Characters/Obeus Yelris\|Obeus]] Yelris, a tall elven man, and even [[Named Characters & Beasts/Important Characters/PC Backstory Characters/Mordecai's Backstory & Family/Claribelle Reverence †\|Claribelle Reverence †]]
- Identity and Origins: He was once a student at [[Locations/Strixhaven/Strixhaven\|Strixhaven]]. He is suspected of working with [[Gods & Heralds/Ignatius/Ignatius\|Ignatius]] and the Ashen Cul. Legend Lore cast on his name yielded the cryptic response that "no one called Ebis Daro" exists, suggesting the name may be an alias.
- Aspects: Ebis creates magical Aspects of himself to hunt his enemies. These constructs can shapeshift and are formidable opponents, but killing them does not harm Ebis himself.
- [[History & Events/The Sundering\|The Sundering]]: [[Named Characters & Beasts/Important Characters/Seifer Willow ‡\|Seifer]] revealed that she and Ebis fought on the same side as commanders during [[History & Events/The Sundering\|the Sundering]], though she found him deeply unpleasant
- Ebis Daro was first encountered by [[Other Information/Display Tags/The Party\|the party]] in the [[Locations/Ehobel/Ishnmel/Wooden Hearth Inn\|Wooden Hearth Inn]] in [[Locations/Ehobel/Ishnmel/Ishnmel\|Ishnmel]] during [[Session Notes/03 The Worst Diplomates/Session 15\|Session 15]], where he introduced himself as the Grandmaster of the [[Groups & Factions/Mage's Guild\|Mage's Guild]].

However, their victory was short-lived. Ebis Daro himself appeared on the battlefield, converting the fallen soldiers into hundreds of new [[Gods & Heralds/Ignatius/Ash Spawn\|Ash Spawn]]. Ebis spared [[The Party/Viktor Malkovich\|Viktor]], dismissing him as insignificant, and departed through a portal. [[Other Information/Display Tags/The Party\|The party]] was left reeling, confronted with the horrifying question of how to defeat an enemy who turns every defeat into fuel for his army
