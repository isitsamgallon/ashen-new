### Overview
A Cloak [[The Party/Mordecai Reverence\|Mordecai Reverence]] bought in a [[Locations/Strixhaven/Strixhaven\|Strixhaven]] magic shop. its a long black mantel which gives off and intense heat which burns any who get too close

### Item Description
When an enemy lands physical attack on the wear. They must roll dexterity saving throw equal to the wears spell save DC. On a failed save they take 1d6 fire damage. 

Immunity to Fire Damage.
