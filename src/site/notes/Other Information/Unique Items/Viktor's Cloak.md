### Overview
A cloak stolen from [[Gods & Heralds/Vaelrith/Vaelrith\|Vaelrith]]'s domain by [[The Party/Viktor Malkovich\|Viktor Malkovich]] in an attempt to cheat death. It housed one of [[Gods & Heralds/Vaelrith/Vaelrith\|Vaelrith]]'s [[Gods & Heralds/Herald\|herald]] within it which was the source of its ability to resurrect and interact with souls. The cloak was destroyed [[Named Characters & Beasts/Important Characters/Ebis Daro\|Ebis Daro]] in his raid on [[Locations/OFC/Blackwall/Blackwall\|Blackwall]] in [[Session Notes/08 Gutenous Guardians/Session 66\|Session 66]] when he freed the [[Gods & Heralds/Herald\|herald]] so it could be added to his [[Gods & Heralds/Ignatius/Ash Spawn\|Ash Spawn]] army.
