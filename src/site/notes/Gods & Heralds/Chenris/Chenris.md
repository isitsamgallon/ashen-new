### Overview & Apotheosis
Chenris is the newest member of [[Gods & Heralds/Gods & Heralds\|The Ashen Pantheon]], achieving h is status amongst the Gods simultaneously during the [[History & Events/Eras/1st Era\|1st]] and [[History & Events/Eras/7th Era\|7th Era]] due to his ascendance taking place in the [[Named Characters & Beasts/Important Characters/Marcel\|Marcel]]'s hidden room which physically was located 1000s of years in the past but happen during the 7th Era chronologically. 

Using incredibly advanced Arithmancy, and the help of [[Other Information/Display Tags/The Party\|The Party]], a young [[Named Characters & Beasts/Important Characters/Marcel\|Marcel]], a young [[Named Characters & Beasts/Important Characters/The Auditors/Niaross †\|Niaross †]] and [[Gods & Heralds/Chronovorus/Chronovorus\|Chronovorus]], [[Gods & Heralds/Chenris/Chenris\|Chenris]] found a way to destroy [[Gods & Heralds/Aeloria ‡/Aeloria ‡\|Aeloria ‡]] and steal her place in the pantheon. For intents and purposes he is now the new God of light and Bringer of Knowledge, having full control over the authorities once held by Aeloria. 

### Divine Attributes
 - Chenris reigns over the divine domains of Light, Knowledge, Hope, and Enlightenment, each facet reflecting her eternal role as the guide of minds and the bearer of sacred truth. Her presence inspires clarity where there was once confusion, and warmth where there was despair.
 - His symbol is a radiant sunburst cradling an open book at its heart, often accompanied by a golden quill encircled by a halo of light - emblems of revelation, learning, and divine inspiration made manifest.
 - The sacred colours of his worship are gold, white, and soft azure: gold for divine wisdom, white for purity of purpose, and azure for the serene depths of understanding.
 - Creatures held dear to him are the golden dove, a messenger of hope; the white stag, a silent guardian and beacon of guidance; and the luminous moth, ever-drawn to the light of hidden truths, fluttering at the threshold between ignorance and knowing.

### Worship & Rituals
#### The illumination Festival 
This grand event takes place during the longest day of the year. Worshippers light countless lanterns and share tales of inspiration and triumph, symbolizing the spreading of knowledge and hope. The festival concludes with a communal vow to pursue truth in all endeavors. 

#### The Ascension of The Mind 
A meditative ritual involving starlight reflection in holy pools, symbolizing enlightenment. Devotees focus their minds on questions plaguing them, often emerging with newfound clarity attributed to Chenris' blessings. 

#### The Oath of Light
Devotees take this sacred oath when joining her clergy or becoming Golden Ones. The initiation involves writing one's life goal on a golden parchment and burning it in her light, signifying surrender to his divine wisdom. 

### Artifacts & Relics
#### Tome of Radiance
A mystical book containing infinite pages, capable of revealing knowledge from any realm. Its inscriptions are written in shifting celestial languages. 

#### The Lantern of Eternal Guidence
A lantern that never extinguishes, offering light in the darkest places. It also reveals hidden truths. 

#### The Quill of illumination
A golden quill that writes truths yet untold, used by his highest priests to record prophecies and divine insights.
